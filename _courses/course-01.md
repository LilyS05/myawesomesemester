## About this course

This course will help students understand the roll of communication in the professional workplace. It will dive into public speaking in corporate contexts. Students will apply interpersonal, intercultural, organization, leadership, and persuasive presentation skills. Additionally this course will guide students through job interviews and teach them how to be the interviewees.  


## About Professor Name

Dr. Angela Widgeon is Professor. Mentor. Problem-Solver. Storyteller. Encourager. She received her PHD at The University of Alabama in Mass Communication/Media Studies. She now teaches several courses at Virginia Tech.
