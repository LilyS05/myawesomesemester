## About this course

This semester I'm taking a business and professional communication course, which covers how communication principles play out specifically in workplace settings — from extemporaneous speaking and presenting (both in-person and virtual) to interpersonal, intercultural, and leadership skills applied through team projects, ethical communication and conflict management, and interviewing strategies from both sides of the table. I'm taking it because it's directly relevant to my career — as a sales intern at McKesson, I'm already presenting, negotiating, and communicating across teams, and this class gives me a more structured way to sharpen those exact skills. My hope for the semester is to walk away more confident presenting on the spot, better at reading and managing team dynamics, and more prepared for interviews I'll do on both sides as I move into a full-time sales role.

## About Professor Name

My professor for Communication Research Methods is Angela Widgeon, who earned her PhD in Mass Communication/Media Studies from The University of Alabama and holds a master's degree from the University of West Florida. Before coming to Virginia Tech, she spent years teaching communication at both Radford University and Liberty University, including several years as an Associate Professor at Liberty. She's been teaching research methods at Virginia Tech's School of Communication since 2023 and also serves on the college's Faculty Council, representing the School of Communication. With that much time in the classroom across multiple universities, she brings a long, varied teaching background to a course that's all about learning how research actually gets done.
### Research interests

Her research interest are discovering and teaching research methods.
