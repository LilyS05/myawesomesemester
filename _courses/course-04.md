## About this course

This semester I'm taking a communication research methods course, which covers how to actually study communication as a discipline — identifying and evaluating existing research, understanding the strengths and weaknesses of different research methods, and learning how to build a literature review and design an original research study of my own. I'm taking it because it's a required course for my major, and because I know that being able to evaluate research critically — rather than just taking studies or statistics at face value — is a skill that carries over directly into sales, where data and claims get thrown around constantly. My hope for the semester is to walk away actually knowing how to design a sound research question and back it up with solid methodology, so I can be a more critical consumer of information and a stronger communicator when I need to make an evidence-based case.
