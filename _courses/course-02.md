## About this course

This semester I'm taking CMST 4714, a senior seminar on how communication is shifting in an AI-transformed world — looking closely at AI-mediated communication while still holding onto my own voice and writing skills. I'm taking it because I'm a senior about to head into sales at McKesson, and I want to actually understand how to use AI thoughtfully instead of just leaning on it. My hope for the semester is to leave with sharper instincts for when AI helps and when it just gets in the way, and stronger communication skills I can trust are genuinely mine.

## About Professor Name

Carlos Evia Puerto, a professor in Virginia Tech's School of Communication who also serves as the college's Associate Dean of Strategic Initiatives and Chief Technology Officer. Beyond his research, he's known for helping shape how the whole college — not just communication majors — thinks about working alongside AI, which makes him a fitting person to be teaching a class built around exactly that question.

### Research interests
His work sits at the intersection of content, technology, and human communication — he studies how automation and AI are changing the way organizations create and manage information, and what skills communicators need to stay essential as that shift happens. 

### Publications

He's written four books on the subject, including Creating Intelligent Content with Lightweight DITA and Content Operations from Start to Scale, and he leads the college's "Tech for Humanity" initiative, which pushes every major in the college to engage seriously with emerging technology.

### Service and outreach

1. Tech for Humanity (TfH) initiative — he leads this cross-disciplinary effort for the whole College of Liberal Arts and Human Sciences, building a "community of practice" among faculty and integrating technology fluency into every CLAHS major, not just Communication.
2. University-level tech governance — as CTO for the college, he represents CLAHS on the VT IT Council and works with the university's Division of Information Technology to align the college's tech priorities with Virginia Tech's broader strategy.
3. IT infrastructure oversight — he supervises a team of IT specialists who manage the college's technology infrastructure for both education and research.
